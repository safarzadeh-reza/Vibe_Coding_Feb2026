Biography

I am a PhD candidate in Geomatics Engineering at the University of Calgary, with a strong record of leading multidisciplinary projects that bridge data science, AI, and geospatial systems. My research focuses on AI-driven spatial optimization, machine learning for transportation systems, and intelligent decision support tools.

Skilled in project management and system integration, I have experience guiding end-to-end lifecycles—from data acquisition to model development, documentation, and stakeholder communication. I design and deliver visualization tools and interactive dashboards that translate complex spatial data into actionable insights.

My work spans collaborations with academic institutions, industry partners, and government agencies, applying advanced machine learning and optimization methods to practical challenges in transportation, urban planning, and environmental systems.
